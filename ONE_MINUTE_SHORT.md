# AxilTree 1-Minute Short (How to Use)

## Format
- Style: Vertical video (9:16)
- Length: 60 seconds
- Goal: Show new users how to set up and share their AxilTree page fast

## Shot-by-shot Script (60s)

### 0:00 - 0:04
**Visual:** Open landing page, quick zoom on logo + hero text.

**Voiceover:**
"Need one link for all your socials? This is AxilTree."

**On-screen text:**
"One bio link for everything"

---

### 0:04 - 0:10
**Visual:** Click **Create Your Page** / go to signup, show quick account creation.

**Voiceover:**
"Sign up in seconds and head straight to your account dashboard."

**On-screen text:**
"Step 1: Sign up"

---

### 0:10 - 0:18
**Visual:** In **Account Settings**, fill full name + bio, upload avatar, click **Update profile**.

**Voiceover:**
"Add your name, bio, and profile photo so people instantly know who you are."

**On-screen text:**
"Step 2: Complete profile"

---

### 0:18 - 0:32
**Visual:** Go to **Links** tab, add Instagram/TikTok/Twitter links, drag to reorder.

**Voiceover:**
"Now add your links—Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, whatever you use—and drag to reorder them."

**On-screen text:**
"Step 3: Add + reorder links"

---

### 0:32 - 0:43
**Visual:** Switch to **Design** tab, change theme, font, animation, username style.

**Voiceover:**
"Customize your page with themes, fonts, and animations to match your vibe."

**On-screen text:**
"Step 4: Make it yours"

---

### 0:43 - 0:52
**Visual:** Open public profile (`/username`) and tap a couple of links.

**Voiceover:**
"Your public page updates live, so every change appears instantly for visitors."

**On-screen text:**
"Live public page"

---

### 0:52 - 1:00
**Visual:** Tap copy icon in header (copy profile link), then paste into Instagram/TikTok bio field.

**Voiceover:**
"Copy your AxilTree link, paste it in your bio, and share everything from one page."

**On-screen text:**
"Done. Share your link everywhere."

## Caption Option (for posting)
"Set up your AxilTree in under a minute: sign up, add links, customize, and share one bio link for everything. #axiltree #linkinbio #creatortools"
